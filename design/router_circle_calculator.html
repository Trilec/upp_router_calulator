Why this works

Template R = hole R + base R − cutter R114.5 = 37.5 + 81 − 4 mm

The cutter centre travels 33.5 mm from the common centre. Its 4 mm cutting radius reaches the finished 37.5 mm hole radius. The 81 mm base radius reaches the 114.5 mm template radius.

Diameter formula & what the result means

Template Ø = hole Ø + base Ø − cutter Ø.
For an existing template: hole R = template R − base R + cutter R.

This sizes the inside opening of the template, not the template board’s outside edge, and not the cutting-tool centreline used to make the template. It predicts the hole’s outer boundary. Routing only the perimeter leaves a central waste piece when the hole is larger than twice the cutter’s diameter.

The guide radius is the distance from the cutter centre to the actual template-contact edge. The diameter calculation assumes that distance is half the entered guide diameter in every direction. A 1 mm change in entered guide diameter changes the calculated template radius by 0.5 mm for the same target hole.

Before treating this as a cutting dimension

The flat side and taper matter. If they change the centre-to-contact distance, the circle can change size. Use only a genuinely concentric circular guide surface; this calculator does not compensate for an irregular base.

Unplug before measuring the cutter or base. Check the contact height and base centring, secure the template and workpiece, keep the router supported, and make a test cut in scrap before cutting the final piece.

Measurement note & manufacturer reference

The 162 mm base and 8 mm cutter defaults are provisional inputs from this conversation, not a dimensional certification. Verify the actual cutting diameter rather than assuming an 8 mm shank also cuts 8 mm.

The Bosch product information checked for the POF 1200 AE did not establish the circular base’s contact diameter. The Bosch product information lists a template-guide accessory; that is distinct from guiding against the outer base edge. This is a geometry calculator, not approval of a particular jig or routing setup.
